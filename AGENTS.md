# Experiment log maintenance

This repository is a lightweight log of experiments, not a formal research archive. Continuously update `README.md` whenever an experiment is added or materially changed.

Keep at most three entries under `# Recent`, newest first. Each entry must include a date and experiment name, exactly three short sentences covering what was tried, the result observed, and the useful takeaway or next question, followed by a real image of that experiment's dashboard or output stored under `assets/`.

When a fourth recent entry is added, move the oldest recent entry under `# Previous experiments` as `## YYYY-MM-DD — Experiment name`, retaining its three sentences and image. Do not invent results or use placeholder images; run the experiment and capture its actual output first.
