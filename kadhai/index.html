What’s evolving?

Each cell is an Algovivo soft body, floating in a gentle current with gravity switched off. Cells grow and absorb the small nutrient particles they touch. Every 30 simulated seconds, the three cells with the most food minus a body-size cost produce the next generation.

This is a rough selection experiment, not a model of biological evolution. Shapes inherit length, width, lobe count and lobe depth. The best genome is preserved; the other offspring mutate. This bounded shape family cannot invent arbitrary anatomy.

Powered by Algovivo. Algovivo handles elasticity and muscle contraction. Drift, nutrient absorption and selection are simple rules added for this experiment; there is no fluid-dynamics solver.
