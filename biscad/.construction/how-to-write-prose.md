# How to write prose (docs, UI copy, commit messages, agent replies)

Write about 80% of the way to ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English. The spec comes from
aerospace maintenance manuals; models know it well, and it reads faster than free prose.

- One instruction or one fact per sentence. Procedural sentences: 20 words or fewer.
  Descriptive sentences: 25 words or fewer.
- Active voice. Imperative mood for instructions: "Set the key." not "The key should be set."
- Simple verbs, one meaning per word: use, get, set, show, make, do, stop, start, keep, send.
- No idioms, no hype, no rhetorical framing ("not X but Y", colon reveals, scare quotes).
- Name the thing the reader sees, not the internal mechanism.
- Units on every number: "0.2 s", "62 mm", "$12/month".
- Paragraphs: 6 sentences or fewer. Lists for sequences; tables for comparisons.

Prefer a diagram, a rendered image or an interactive page to a long text explanation. A picture
of the part beats a paragraph about the part.
